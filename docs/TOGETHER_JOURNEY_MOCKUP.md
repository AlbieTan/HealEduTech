# Explore → Together → avatar growth mockup

**Branch:** `explore+together+avatargrowthdemo`  
**Status:** Interactive design preview. No new backend tables, rewards, verified activity, saved avatar state, or community publishing.

## Walkthrough

1. Open `/explore`, expand any of the three sample activities, and select **Try this together**.
2. Together opens with the matching nature, stories, or science challenge. The topic pills let you switch challenges.
3. The three sample buttons illustrate trying, comparing, and explaining. Points move from 0 → 1 → 3 → 6 and the existing pixel companion grows. Reset starts the sample again; changing topic resets its steps.
4. The existing page-only project form illustrates a private draft and fictional facilitator feedback. The fictional showcases show a possible reviewed community space.

The revised Together page puts the journey in this order: choose a challenge, see progress, record a discovery, get inspired. A learner can also browse related books for the selected topic. English and Vietnamese copy cover the new journey.

## Product boundary

Explore currently displays approved sample activities, but opening them is not a completion event. The Together buttons award sample points on click only. The project form is memory-only and does not submit a project. Live reading history and learner interests exist elsewhere in the site, but this mockup does not read them or expose private reflections. A real implementation would need a deliberate activity record, learner consent and visibility controls, facilitator review, moderation, and defined recognition rules before connecting points to stored progress.
